# How to read Armenian: the reading rules in 10 minutes
> Armenian is read almost exactly as it is written. Here are all the exceptions: ե and ո at the start of a word, ու, և, the invisible ը sound and stress.
Good news: Armenian spelling is almost phonetic. Once you know the letters, you can read words. There are only a few exceptions, all listed here.
## 1. Ե and Ո at the start of a word
**ե** at the start of a word sounds "ye", inside a word "e". **ո** at the start sounds "vo", inside "o".
{{words: երեկ, երեխա, ով}}
**ով** ("who") is an exception: it is read "ov".
## 2. ՈՒ is "u"
The two letters **ու** together make one sound, "u". Armenian has no single letter for "u".
{{words: տուն, ջուր, լուսին}}
## 3. և is "ev" or "yev"
**և** is **ե** + **վ**: "yev" at the start of a word, "ev" inside. On its own, **և** means "and".
{{words: Երևան, արև}}
## 4. The invisible ը
Between consonants Armenian often adds a short neutral vowel that is not written: **գրել** sounds "gəɾel", **մկրատ** sounds "məkrat".
{{words: գրել, սպասել}}
## 5. Aspiration
The pairs **տ/թ**, **պ/փ**, **կ/ք**, **ճ/չ**, **ծ/ց** differ by a puff of air: the second letter is followed by a light "h". Barik marks it with **ʰ**: **թեյ** — [tʰey].
## 6. Two r's
**Ր** is a soft r, **Ռ** is rolled. **Ղ** is a guttural sound, like a French r.
## 7. Stress
Stress almost always falls on the **last syllable**: Yereván, shnorhakalutyún. If the last syllable has the invisible ը, the stress moves one syllable back.
