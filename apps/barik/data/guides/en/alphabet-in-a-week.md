# Learn the Armenian alphabet in a week: a day-by-day plan
> How to learn all 39 Armenian letters in 7 days, 15 minutes a day: which letters to take each day, how to memorise them and how to test yourself.
The Armenian alphabet was created by Mesrop Mashtots in 405 AD. It had 36 letters; **Օ** and **Ֆ** were added later, and Eastern Armenian also counts **ու** and **և** as letters — 39 in total. That sounds like a lot, but almost every letter always sounds the same, so 15 minutes a day for a week is enough.
## How to practise
- Each day: 5–6 new letters plus yesterday's.
- A letter is three things: its shape, its name and its sound. Say each one out loud.
- Write by hand — handwriting sticks better than just looking. In Barik you can trace every letter with your finger.
- Read real words straight away: a letter without a word is quickly forgotten.
## Day 1. Letters that look friendly
**Ա ա** (a), **Մ մ** (m), **Ն ն** (n), **Ս ս** (s), **Կ կ** (k), **Լ լ** (l). You can already read:
{{words: մայր, քաղաք, լավ}}
## Day 2. Vowels
**Ե ե** (e, "ye" at the start of a word), **Է է** (e), **Ի ի** (i), **Ո ո** (o, "vo" at the start), **ՈՒ ու** (u), **Օ օ** (o).
## Day 3. Voiced and voiceless pairs
**Բ բ** (b) and **Պ պ** (p), **Գ գ** (g) and **Կ կ** (k), **Դ դ** (d) and **Տ տ** (t), **Զ զ** (z), **Վ վ** (v).
## Day 4. Aspirated consonants
Armenian has voiceless sounds with a puff of air: **Թ թ** (tʰ), **Փ փ** (pʰ), **Ք ք** (kʰ), **Չ չ** (chʰ), **Ց ց** (tsʰ). Say "t" and breathe out right after it — that is **թ**.
{{words: քույր, թեյ}}
## Day 5. Hissing sounds
**Շ շ** (sh), **Ժ ժ** (zh), **Ճ ճ** (ch), **Ջ ջ** (j), **Ծ ծ** (ts), **Ձ ձ** (dz).
## Day 6. Special sounds
**Խ խ** (kh, as in "loch"), **Ղ ղ** (gh, like a French r), **Հ հ** (h), **Ռ ռ** (rolled r) and **Ր ր** (soft r), **Յ յ** (y), **Ֆ ֆ** (f), **Ը ը** (a short neutral vowel).
## Day 7. Review
Go through the whole [alphabet](/en/a/), hide the hints and name every letter. Then read ten words in a row — if you can, the alphabet is yours.
