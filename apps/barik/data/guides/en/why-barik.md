# Barik: how it differs from a dictionary or a translator
> A dictionary or a translator gives you a translation. Barik teaches: letters with handwriting, words with spaced repetition, examples with audio, a path from the first letters to reading Tumanyan.
A dictionary answers "how do I say this". A translator answers "what does this mean". Neither helps you **remember**. Barik is built so that Armenian stays with you.
## What is inside
- **The alphabet with handwriting.** 39 letters: sound, name and handwriting you can trace with your finger.
- **20,000 Eastern Armenian words** translated into six languages, with pronunciation and audio. Synthetic Microsoft Azure voices — female and male.
- **Spaced repetition.** Words come back just as you start forgetting them: after a day, two, four, a week, two weeks, a month.
- **The Pomegranate path.** From letters to phrases and on to reading original texts by Tumanyan, Terian and Charents.
- **A phrasebook** — almost 300 ready-made phrases with audio.
- **Works offline.** The site works without internet and can be added to your phone's home screen like an app.
## Price
The alphabet, level A1, the first steps of the path, the dictionary and pronunciation are free. The rest is by subscription.
