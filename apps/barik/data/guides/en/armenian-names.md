# Armenian names and their meanings
> How popular names are written in Armenian and what they mean: Anahit, Arpi, Lusine, Hayk, Tigran, Narek. And how to write your own name in Armenian letters.
Many Armenian names are ordinary words — "sun", "moon", "rose", "star". Know the word and you understand the name.
## Names that are words
- **Լուսինե** (Lusine) — from **լուսին**, "moon".
- **Արփի** (Arpi) — a poetic word for "sun, light".
- **Աստղիկ** (Astghik) — "little star", from **աստղ**; also an ancient goddess of love.
- **Վարդ** (Vard) and **Վարդուհի** — from **վարդ**, "rose".
- **Գոհար** (Gohar) — "jewel".

## Names from history
- **Հայկ** (Hayk) — the legendary forefather of the Armenians.
- **Տիգրան** (Tigran) — King Tigranes the Great.
- **Անահիտ** (Anahit) — an ancient patron goddess.
- **Նարեկ** (Narek) — after the Narekavank monastery and the poet Grigor Narekatsi.
- **Մեսրոպ** (Mesrop) — Mesrop Mashtots, creator of the alphabet.
## Surnames in -յան
The ending **-յան** (-yan, -ian) means "of the family of": **Պետրոսյան** — "of Petros's family". Most Armenian surnames are built this way.
## Write your own name
Armenian letters show sounds more precisely than Latin ones: each sound has its own letter. Barik has [name pages](/en/n/) — your name in big Armenian letters and in handwriting — and in the app a pen writes any name by hand, which you can save as a card.
