# The first 100 Armenian words for life in Armenia
> One hundred words to start with if you have moved to Armenia or visit often: people, food, the city, money, time and the key verbs.
These words cover most everyday situations: the shop, a taxi, a café, meeting people. Press the button to hear a word; tap the word to open its page with an example and its forms.
